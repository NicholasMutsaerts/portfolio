Phishing attacks continue to evolve, using increasingly sophisticated techniques to deceive users and steal sensitive information such as credentials, financial data, and personal information. Mitigating these risks requires a combination of user awareness, secure processes, and robust technology controls.

---

## Best Practices for Prevention

### 1. Verify Email Authenticity
Exercise caution with unsolicited communications. Carefully review sender addresses, domains, and message content for inconsistencies or anomalies.

### 2. Avoid Suspicious Links and Attachments
Do not click on unfamiliar links or download unexpected attachments. Always preview URLs before interacting to confirm legitimacy.

### 3. Implement Ongoing Security Awareness Training
Provide regular cybersecurity training to employees, with phishing awareness included in onboarding and reinforced at least semi-annually. Effective training strengthens the organization’s ability to recognize and respond to threats.

### 4. Enforce Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
Require MFA across systems and applications. This adds a critical layer of protection, significantly reducing the risk of unauthorized access in the event of credential compromise.

### 5. Deploy Anti-Phishing and Endpoint Protection
Use enterprise-grade email filtering and endpoint protection tools to detect and block malicious content. Ensure antivirus and anti-malware solutions are properly configured and regularly updated.

### 6. Scrutinize Urgent or Unusual Requests
Phishing attacks often create a sense of urgency to prompt immediate action. Independently verify any requests involving sensitive data, financial transactions, or account changes.

### 7. Leverage Email Security Features
Educate users on how to:

- Mark messages as junk or phishing  
- Block suspicious senders  
- Apply email filtering rules  

These actions help reduce exposure to recurring threats.

### 8. Maintain Up-to-Date Systems
Keep operating systems, applications, and security tools fully updated. Regular patching reduces vulnerabilities that attackers may exploit.
