### IT Guide: How to Change Your Profile Picture in Microsoft Teams (and Microsoft 365)

Your Microsoft Teams profile picture helps coworkers identify you in chats, meetings, and channels. This guide explains how to change, remove, or update your profile picture in Microsoft Teams. There are the two most common ways your profile photo is managed at work:

1. Change it directly in **Microsoft Teams**.
2. Change it in **Microsoft 365/My Account**, which then appears across **Microsoft 365 apps** (including Teams).

---

### Change Your Profile Picture with Microsoft Teams

- Open Microsoft Teams
- Select your **profile picture/initials** in the **top-right**.
- When the account panel opens, select your **profile picture** again.
- Select **Upload** (to choose a new photo) or **Remove** (to delete the current photo).
- Select **Save** to apply changes.

**Important note:** Microsoft states it may take time for your Teams profile picture to update everywhere, and **clearing Teams cache may help it update quicker**.

---

### Change your profile picture in Microsoft 365 / My Account (Work or school account)

**Microsoft 365 (Microsoft365.com)**

- Sign in at [waMicrosoft365.com](Microsoft365.com) with your work/school account.
- Select the profile circle (top-right) showing your initials/photo.
- In the My accounts pane, select the profile circle again. 
- Choose Change your photo, upload your image, and save. 

**My Microsoft Account Portal**
- Sign in to [myaccount.microsoft.com](myaccount.microsoft.com) with your work/school account.
- Select the **Edit** icon on your current photo.
- Upload the new photo, resize if needed, then select **Save**.
